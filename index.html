BACKGROUND

About

I'm Sohum, a high school engineer based in British Columbia. My work sits at the intersection of embedded firmware, electronics, and physical systems. I'm drawn to problems where all three have to work together perfectly.

What started with curiosity about how robots move led me to build a minimal-DOF quadruped. Asking how bioelectrical signals could be better controlled led me to a research investigation into closed-loop TENS stimulation. I build to understand, and I document everything along the way.
